    FAQ

    Questions about your park map

    What does a custom park map cost?

    Pricing is $299–$499 per park map, depending on the size of your park and your special requests. You receive a quote tailored to your park before any work starts.

    Which types of parks do you design maps for?

    Amusement and theme parks, safari and drive-thru parks, zoos and wildlife parks, waterparks and adventure parks such as zipline, ropes or go-kart parks. Every map is designed from your own grounds, attractions and branding.

    What do you need from us to get started?

    Your current map or site plan (a screenshot or photo is enough), a short description of what should be improved, and your logo or brand colors if you have them.

    How does the process work?

    You send us your current map and your wishes, we send you a quote, and once you approve it, we design the map and deliver it in the formats you need.

    How long does it take?

    It depends on the size of your park and the options you choose. We confirm the timeline together with your quote.

    Which formats do we receive?

    One design covers every use you choose: a large entrance board, a folding map to take along, and a digital version for your website, app and screens in the park.

    Can we request changes?

    Yes. You review the design before final delivery, and the feedback rounds are agreed in your quote.

    Can you make seasonal or Spanish versions?

    Yes. Four seasonal versions and multiple languages, for example English and Spanish, are optional add-ons in the package builder above.

    Is there any obligation when we ask for a quote?

    No. A quote request is free and without obligation.

    Show us your map. We will show you the upgrade.

    There is no obligation. Briefly describe what should be improved or redone, and send the request directly. It opens as an email draft, so you can attach your current map.

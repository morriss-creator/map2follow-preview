Benefits

Better orientation, easier coordination

A live map helps your guests find their way and stay together, from the parking lot to the last ride of the day.

Find attractions faster

Guests tap a ride, show or animal area and see right away where it is in the park.

Without it: Guests turn a paper plan in circles or ask the staff.

Facilities at a glance

Restrooms, food, first aid and parking are one tap away, filtered by category.

Without it: Facilities are hard to spot on a busy plan.

Easier coordination for groups

Families and groups agree on a meeting point on the map and plan their day together.

Without it: Phone calls asking where everyone is, and searching across the park.

Less confusion at the entrance

Fewer repeated questions at the entrance and the info desk, because the answers are on the guests' phones.

Without it: Lines at the info desk with the same questions.

Link or QR code, no app

Guests open the map via a link or a QR code in their phone's browser. Nothing to install.

Without it: Apps that guests are reluctant to download.

Know where you are, no GPS needed

Numbered signposts in the park match the map. Their QR codes open the map with a "You are here" marker and the way to the next ride, restroom or meeting point.

Without it: Guests are not sure where they are on the plan.

FAQ

Questions about your live park map

What is a live park map?

A realistic, interactive map of your park that your guests open on their phones. They search and tap rides, food, restrooms and meeting points, follow suggested routes and coordinate with their family or group during the visit.

What is the free preview sketch?

Before you pay anything, we create a free preview sketch of your park as a live map, based on your current map or site plan. It shows you how your map could look and work. It is free and without obligation; afterwards you decide if you want to order.

What if we don't like the first draft?

Then you don't pay. Our first-draft guarantee: if you are not happy with the first draft, you can stop there and owe nothing.

How does payment work?

Typically 50% after you approve the first draft and 50% on delivery. The exact terms are confirmed in your quote. Every package is a one-time price, with no subscription and no ongoing fees.

Is it a subscription?

No. Every package is a one-time purchase: one-time price, no subscription and no ongoing fees.

How do guests open the map?

Via a link or a QR code, for example at the entrance, on tickets, on signposts or on your website. The map opens in the phone's web browser, so there is no app to download.

How do guests know where they are without GPS?

With numbered signposts in your park that match the map. Each signpost has its own QR code: scanning it opens the map with a "You are here" marker at that spot, so guests see where they are and the suggested route to where they want to go. Signature includes up to 10 signpost QR codes, Premium up to 20.

What does a live park map cost?

Essential costs $299, Signature $399 and Premium $499 per park map, each as a one-time price. The final price depends on the size of your park and your special requests, and is confirmed in your quote.

Which package is right for our park?

Essential suits smaller parks with up to 15 clickable points and an entrance QR code. Signature adds up to 35 points, up to 10 signpost QR codes, search, filters, info pop-ups with photos and website embedding, which is why we recommend it for most parks. Premium adds up to 50 points and 20 signpost QR codes, English and Spanish, a seasonal or event layer, meeting points and suggested routes, and two update rounds within 12 months.

What is in the launch kit?

Every package includes a launch kit: print-ready QR code sign templates (PDF) for your entrance and signposts, a ready-to-share link, a social media preview image and post graphic, and a short setup guide for your staff.

What is the difference between minor updates and update rounds?

Minor updates are small corrections such as a changed opening time, a renamed place or a moved pin. They are free for 30 days with Essential, 90 days with Signature and 12 months with Premium. Update rounds are larger changes, for example adding a new attraction; Premium includes two update rounds within 12 months. Further updates are available on request.

What is the offer until December 31, 2026?

If you book any package before December 31, 2026, you get a winter or holiday layer for your map for free. With Premium, it comes in addition to the included seasonal or event layer.

Where is the map hosted, and can we embed it on our website?

Your live map is a one-time purchase. How it is hosted and embedded on your website is agreed in your quote. Website embedding is part of the Signature and Premium packages.

Which devices does it work on?

On smartphones, tablets and computers with a current web browser. The map is optimized for phones, because that is where guests use it in the park.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of your park and the package you choose. We give you a turnaround estimate with your quote. Premium includes priority handling.

What do you need from us to get started?

Your current map or site plan (a screenshot or photo is enough), a list of your attractions and facilities, and your logo or brand colors. For info pop-ups we also use your photos and short descriptions.

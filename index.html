FAQ

Questions about your park map

What does a custom park map cost?

We offer three packages: Essential for $299, Signature for $399 and Premium for $499 per park map. The final price depends on the size of your park and your special requests, and is confirmed in your quote before any work starts.

Which package is right for our park?

Essential suits smaller parks with up to 15 labeled points. Signature adds up to 30 points, a facility legend, signage and folding map files and a mobile web version, which is why we recommend it for most parks. Premium adds a bilingual version, an interactive web map, a seasonal or event variant and the source files.

Which types of parks do you design maps for?

Amusement and theme parks, safari and drive-thru parks, zoos and wildlife parks, waterparks and adventure parks such as zipline, ropes or go-kart parks. Every map is designed from your own grounds, attractions and branding.

What do you need from us to get started?

Your current map or site plan (a screenshot or photo is enough), a short description of what should be improved, and your logo or brand colors if you have them.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of your park and the package you choose. We confirm the timeline together with your quote. Premium includes priority handling.

How many revisions are included?

Essential includes 2 revision rounds, Signature and Premium include 3 revision rounds.

Which file formats do we receive?

Every package includes a print-ready PDF and web images (PNG/JPG). Signature adds large-format files for signage, a folding brochure version and a mobile-optimized web version. Premium also includes the source files.

Can you make Spanish or seasonal versions?

Yes. Premium includes a bilingual version (for example English and Spanish) and one seasonal or event variant. Additional languages and seasonal versions are available as add-ons, priced on request.

Is there any obligation when we ask for a quote?

No. A quote request is free and without obligation.

Show us your map. We will show you the upgrade.

There is no obligation. Briefly describe what should be improved or redone, and send the request directly. It opens as an email draft, so you can attach your current map.
